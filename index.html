Differential Analysis of the Infusion Curve:
• Curve Fitting: Smooth mathematical polynomial representation $P(t)$ fitted to recorded clinical discrete intervals.
• First Derivative ($dP/dt$): Identifies the instantaneous rate of pressure change. Peak $dP/dt$ occurs at the transition point when compliance is stressed.
• Second Derivative ($d^2P/dt^2$): Identifies inflection dynamics ($d^2P/dt^2 = 0$), delineating the transition from rapid expansion to compensatory resorption plateau.
• Resistance to Outflow ($R_{out}$): $(P_p - P_0) / I_c$. Values > 12 mmHg/(mL/min) predict positive response to CSF diversion.
